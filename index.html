## commands

 violent-bot — slash commands
/help
Re-sends the welcome message and the getting-started information.
/setup world
Creates the channels for a world and subscribes to its events.
/repair world
Re-creates any channel you deleted or unintentionally broke.
/remove world
Deletes the channels and roles created for a world, and unsubscribes from its events.
/settings
One panel for everything a tracked world shows — minimum levels, the online list layout, which deaths and levels appear, exiva lists and the fullbless threshold.
/hunted
Manage the hunted list.
/allies
Manage the allies list.
/stamina
Show your respawn claim stamina and what's currently using it.
/bookings
Show the respawn slots you have booked.
/lootsplit
Split a party hunt — paste your hunt analyser and it does the arithmetic.
/galthen satchel
Sets a Galthen’s Satchel cooldown timer and messages you when it expires.
/boosted option
Filter the boosted boss and creature notifications, or turn them off.
/patreon
View or manage your own Patreon seats.

## privacy & terms

References

When referring to data, it means Discord info that is stored to retain settings and user configuration.

Collection of data

A Discord guild and the ids of the channels, categories and roles that the bot creates are stored in a database.

Distribution of data

None of your data is being distributed to anybody else.

Deletion of data

All data for your guild is deleted from the database when the bot is kicked from your Discord server.
