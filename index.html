## channels

Run it combined for a single online channel, or separate for dedicated channels for allies, enemies and neutrals. The channel name carries the count and refreshes about every 6 minutes.

Every death on the server, marked by whether the character was an enemy, an ally or a neutral, and whether it was a PvE death or a PvP kill. Add /exiva to allied deaths and killer names become copy-pasteable.

Tracks who joined which guild, who left, and who quietly changed their name.

Set up respawn claims for your server so everyone can schedule hunts ahead of time. It's a forum — every respawn gets its own post, showing at a glance whether it's free or claimed.

One combined channel for advancements, with a minimum-level filter so low-level noise never reaches it.

Pokes when someone dies to a rare nemesis boss, an enemy dies fullbless, my galthen satchel cooldown expires, or the boosted boss and creature change at server save.

Every command run through the bot is logged here — as is every enemy the bot detected on its own and added to the hunted list.

## commands

 violent-bot — slash commands
/help
Re-sends the getting-started message the bot posts on join.
/stamina
Show your respawn claim stamina and what's currently using it.
/bookings
Show the respawn slots you have booked.
/setup world
Creates the channels and subscribes to that world's events.
/remove world
Deletes the channels and roles created for a world, and unsubscribes from its events.
/allies list
Manage the allies list.
/hunted list
Manage the hunted list.
/filter levels deaths
Put a minimum level on the levels or deaths channel to keep low-level spam out.
/fullbless level
Minimum level at which a fullbless enemy death pokes you.
/online list world
Toggle the online list between separate (three channels) and combined (one).
/neutral deaths levels
Show, hide or tag neutral events in the levels and deaths channels.
/exiva deaths
Adds the exiva list to allied deaths — bigger posts, easily copied killer names.
/repair world
Re-creates any channel you deleted or unintentionally broke.
/galthen satchel
Notifies you when your satchel cooldowns expire.
/boosted list
Configure your boosted boss and creature notifications.

## privacy & terms

References

When referring to data, it means Discord info that is stored to retain settings and user configuration.

Collection of data

A Discord guild and the ids of the channels, categories and roles that the bot creates are stored in a database.

Distribution of data

None of your data is being distributed to anybody else.

Deletion of data

All data for your guild is deleted from the database when the bot is kicked from your Discord server.
